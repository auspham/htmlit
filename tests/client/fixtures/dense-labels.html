A subgraph hub whose many labelled edges must each keep their own label

A node inside a subgraph fans labelled edges out to targets in several directions - the shape that used to mis-bind a label to the wrong edge, because the label-to-edge match compared positions in mismatched coordinate frames and assigned greedily. Each label must stay bound to its own edge, so dragging one target moves only that edge's label.
